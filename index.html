Client questions

Frequently asked questions

We already have extinguishers. Why do we need a fire alarm?

An extinguisher works only when someone knows there is a fire. A fire alarm system gives the early warning — it detects fire at the initial stage and alerts everyone, even at night or in unattended rooms. For most commercial and institutional buildings it is also an NBC and Fire NOC requirement.

Abhi fire alarm ki zarurat nahi hai — why now?

Delay in detecting a fire is the biggest risk. Most damage happens in the first few minutes, before anyone notices. A fire alarm buys those critical minutes for evacuation and response.

How much does it cost?

It depends on building area & height, number of devices, panel type (conventional / addressable) and cable routing. We do a FREE site survey and give you a clear, itemised quotation — a life-safety and property-protection investment, not just a compliance cost.

Conventional or addressable — which one do I need?

Conventional suits smaller premises like shops, clinics and small offices. Addressable is better for hospitals, hotels, malls, factories and campuses because it pinpoints the exact device. Our engineer recommends the right one after the survey.

Our system gives false alarms / doesn't work. Can you fix it?

Yes. We inspect the panel, detectors and wiring, find the root cause (wrong detector type, dust, wiring faults, battery issues) and repair or upgrade the system — then test everything again.

Do you help with Fire NOC?

Our design follows NBC Part 4, IS 2189 and local fire authority requirements, and you receive complete testing & handover documents. We also offer Fire NOC consultancy for end-to-end support.

How long does installation take?

A small office takes a few days. Schools, hotels, hospitals and factories are project-based — it depends on device quantity, cable routing and site readiness. You get a clear timeline with the proposal.

Do you provide AMC after installation?

Yes — regular detector testing, panel and battery checks with service reports, plus fire audits, staff training and mock drills.
