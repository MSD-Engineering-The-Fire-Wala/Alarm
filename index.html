Questions

Before you book

Still unsure? Call us on 77720 78610 — our engineer will guide you.

We already have extinguishers. Why a fire alarm?

An extinguisher only helps once someone knows there is a fire. A fire alarm detects it early and alerts everyone — even at night or in empty rooms. It is also required for Fire NOC in most commercial buildings.

How much does a fire alarm system cost?

It depends on building area, number of devices, panel type and cable routing. After a FREE site survey you get a clear, itemised quotation.

Our system gives false alarms / doesn't work. Can you fix it?

Yes. We check the panel, detectors and wiring, find the cause and repair or upgrade the system — then test everything again.

Will this help with Fire NOC?

Our design follows NBC Part 4, IS 2189 and local fire norms, and you receive full testing & handover documents. We also offer Fire NOC consultancy.

Do you provide AMC after installation?

Yes — regular detector testing, panel & battery checks with service reports.
