Smoke Detectors

Heat Detectors

Response Indicators

Common questions

Fire alarm — your questions answered

We already have fire extinguishers. Do we still need a fire alarm?

Yes. An extinguisher works only when someone knows there is a fire. A fire alarm system gives the early warning — it detects fire at the initial stage and alerts everyone, even at night or in unattended rooms.

Abhi fire alarm ki zarurat nahi hai — why now?

Delay in detecting a fire is the single biggest risk. Most fire damage happens in the first few minutes, before anyone notices. A fire alarm buys those critical minutes for evacuation and response — and it's also required for Fire NOC and insurance in most commercial buildings.

How much does a fire alarm system cost?

Cost depends on building area & height, number of detectors, panel capacity, cable quantity and routing. Instead of a fixed price, we do a free site survey and give you a transparent, itemised quote. Think of it as a life-safety and property-protection investment — not just a compliance cost.

Conventional or addressable — which one do I need?

Conventional systems suit smaller premises like shops, clinics and small offices. Addressable systems are better for hospitals, hotels, malls, factories and campuses as they pinpoint the exact device in alarm. Our engineer will recommend the right one after the survey.

How long does installation take?

A small office can be done in a few days. Schools, hotels, hospitals and factories are project-based — duration depends on device quantity, cable routing complexity and site readiness. You get a clear timeline with the proposal.

Will this help us get a Fire NOC?

Our design follows NBC Part 4, IS 2189 and local fire authority requirements, and you receive full testing & handover documentation. NOC approval itself is a separate service — we also offer Fire NOC consultancy if you need end-to-end support.

What is not included in the installation scope?

Civil work, core cutting, electrical infrastructure modification, internet integration, Fire NOC approval and third-party certification are outside the standard scope — we'll clearly mention any such requirement in your proposal.

Do you provide maintenance after installation?

Yes. We offer Fire Alarm AMC with regular detector testing, panel checks, battery checks and service reports — plus fire safety audits, staff training and mock fire drills.
